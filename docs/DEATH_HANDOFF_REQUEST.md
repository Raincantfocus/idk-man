# DEATH ANIMATION -> RAGDOLL HANDOFF WITH COLLISION AWARENESS
Engine: Godot [exact version]. Verify every API against it (PhysicalBoneSimulator3D,
PhysicalBone3D, PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D, AnimationTree, SkeletonModifier3D
influence). Never invent APIs.

## GOAL
Death clips must never phase through world geometry. A dying enemy plays its death
animation, then hands off to ragdoll at the clip's planned point, OR hands off
immediately if the animation is blocked or disturbed. No teleport pops and no
visible limbs inside walls, floors, props or other bodies.

## 1. DEATH CLIP DATA (one Resource per clip, JSON-exportable)
id, direction (front/back/left/right), root_motion_distance, handoff_time (planned
ragdoll start), ends_lying (bool), key_bones (head, chest, pelvis, both hands, both
feet, both knees), clearance_samples (key-bone positions in character space every
0.1 s, generated by a script from the clip itself), swept_capsule_radius per bone,
min_clearance_behind (cm), priority. Provide the script that bakes this data from
every supplied death clip, and tell me which clips lack usable data.

## 2. BEFORE THE CLIP PLAYS: PICK A CLIP THAT FITS
- On the lethal hit, compute the hit-relative direction, then test candidate clips
  from the clearance_samples against the world: world transform of the samples ->
  PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D shape queries (collide_shape / cast_motion) using
  capsules at the key bones, against the world layer.
- Pick the highest-priority clip that is clear. If none are clear, use a
  "ragdoll-now" death: a short flinch (<= 0.15 s) blended straight into ragdoll.
- Never choose a clip and "hope". Log the choice and the reason in debug builds.

## 3. WHILE THE CLIP PLAYS: WATCH FOR OBSTRUCTION
Run every physics tick, only while an enemy is in its death animation:
- ROOT MOTION: apply root motion through move_and_collide / a body check, not
  by writing the transform. If the body moves < [60]% of the expected distance
  for [3] consecutive ticks, trigger ragdoll (reason: root_blocked).
- KEY BONES: for each key bone, sweep from its previous-frame position to its
  current position (cast_motion or a ray plus a small shape) so fast limbs
  cannot tunnel through thin walls. Any hit on the world layer or a dynamic prop
  triggers ragdoll (reason: bone_blocked). Cheaper alternative if profiling shows
  the sweeps are too costly: Area3D on BoneAttachment3D nodes plus a ray from the
  last position; state which you chose and the measured cost.
- NO SUPPORT: ray down from the pelvis and feet; if there is no floor within
  [30] cm (ledge, stairs, pit), trigger ragdoll (reason: no_support).
- DISTURBANCE: a second lethal or heavy hit, explosion above [impulse threshold],
  a moving prop or physics body hitting the enemy, or another body pushing it
  triggers ragdoll (reason: disturbed). Explosions also give the impulse to apply.
- PLANNED: at handoff_time, trigger ragdoll (reason: planned).

## 4. THE HANDOFF (must not pop or explode)
1. Capture the current skeleton pose and per-bone linear/angular velocity from the
   last 2-3 frames of the animated pose (finite differences).
2. If the trigger was obstruction, depenetrate: use collide_shape on each key bone
   and push penetrating bones out along the contact normal by the contact depth
   plus a small margin BEFORE starting simulation. Never leave bones inside static
   geometry when the simulation begins.
3. Start the PhysicalBoneSimulator3D (use the full body, or the partial bone list
   for a gentle handoff) and blend with influence from 0 to 1 over [0.12-0.25] s
   instead of a hard snap, if your version supports it. Otherwise use the
   pose-capture approach and say so.
4. Apply the captured velocities, clamped to a per-bone maximum, plus the hit or
   explosion impulse clamped to a maximum. Nobody should fly across the arena.
5. Stop the AnimationTree from writing to the skeleton's body bones once the
   blend finishes. Keep facial or gear secondary animation only if it is safe.
6. Set up collision layers/masks so the ragdoll collides with world and props,
   and does NOT block the player capsule in a way that breaks wallrun, slide or
   mantle. State the layers and masks.

## 5. BUDGET AND FALLBACKS
- Ragdoll budget is [6] full ragdolls near the player. If the budget is full when a
  trigger fires, never fall back to phasing: first evict the oldest or farthest
  ragdoll to a frozen pose; if no slot can be freed, use a LITE ragdoll (<= 8
  bodies: head, chest, pelvis, 2 arms, 2 legs) that copies back to the full
  skeleton. State the cost of each.
- Settle: sleep thresholds, damping, then freeze to a static pose. A frozen corpse
  keeps its wound caps and detached parts, and counts toward the corpse and gib caps.
- Detached limbs and severed pieces get their own bodies and the same
  no-phasing rules.

## 6. DO NOT CHANGE GAMEPLAY
Exactly one authoritative death event per enemy, fired once at the lethal hit and
never re-fired by the ragdoll handoff. Loot, score and wave counts must not change
with the trigger reason. Use the seeded cosmetic RNG, never the gameplay RNG.
Pause and Focus use world time. Gore Off/Reduced/Full never changes the handoff
behavior.

## 7. TEST HARNESS AND ACCEPTANCE
Build a test scene that spawns dying enemies in these cases, each with every death
direction: standing against a wall; 20 cm from a wall; next to a crate; in a
corridor; at a ledge and on stairs; hit by an explosion toward a pillar; two
enemies adjacent; a corpse already behind the enemy; shot a second time mid-death;
a prop pushed into the enemy mid-death; at max ragdoll budget.
Per run, log the trigger reason, the handoff time, the max penetration depth per key
bone per frame, and any teleport larger than [1] cm at the handoff.
PASS: no key bone more than 2 cm inside static geometry for more than 2 frames; no
handoff pop above [1] cm; zero phasing in every case above; stable ragdolls (no
jitter) with measured frame cost; the one-death-event rule verified by a test.
Provide screenshots or a short capture of the worst three cases. Do not claim a pass
without the logs.
