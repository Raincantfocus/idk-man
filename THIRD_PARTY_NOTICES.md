## Animated elite and additional surfaces — CC BY 3.0

The armored robotic elite, its idle/run/flinch clips, armor textures, and anti-slip/riveted/technical panel maps use the same pinned TPS Demo source and attribution above. Character geometry and skin are retained; three animations are selected and unused animation buffers removed. Runtime scale, orientation, materials, playback, and a frozen-pose corpse attachment are adapted for ZERO-LINE. Additional source textures are unchanged; anti-slip normal/ORM files are renamed locally for consistent material loading.

Source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in [assets/elite/provenance.json](assets/elite/provenance.json). Reproduce with `python tools/import_phase2_character.py`. The original complete CC BY notice accompanies all packages.

## Original first-person weapon art

The fictional carbine, box-fed shotgun, sidearm, glove/sleeve meshes, and four-bone first-person rig are original project content generated by `tools/author_first_person.py`. The fabric shader and handling pose curves are authored for this project. `assets/first_person/provenance.json` records their source and geometry hashes. Weapon metal materials reuse the licensed TPS Demo metal normal map already credited above; no external firearm or hand models were copied.

## Original operator character art

The human enforcer, rusher, shield, flusher, and elite meshes and shared 15-bone rig are original project content generated by `tools/author_operators.py`. Runtime skeletal pose curves are authored for this project. `assets/operators/provenance.json` records geometry hashes and triangle counts. Armor reuses the TPS Demo painted-metal albedo/ORM and default-metal normal textures credited above; cloth uses the original first-person fabric shader. No external human models or animation clips were copied for these four variants.

Version 0.7 replaces the displayed robotic elite with original human soldier geometry. The older licensed elite asset remains in source for historical reproducibility; its attribution remains above. Human facial geometry, skin materials, and dismemberment cut meshes are original project content.

https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482

The bundled graphical data is released under CC0 1.0 Universal; source copyright notices are retained. The MakeHuman application source is separately licensed AGPL and is not bundled or executed.
