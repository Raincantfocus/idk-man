CONCEPT


Map Overview
The map is a single huge warehouse shell, 100 meters wide and 70 meters deep, capped with two long strip skylights that
cast bright daylight pools on the action zone. Inside, a classic CoD 3-lane layout runs north-south: the west lane is close-
quarters crate-and-pallet aisles, the mid lane is a long open sightline anchored by a central mid tower, and the east lane is a
vehicle lane with trucks and concrete barriers. Team Alpha spawns on the north short side, Team Bravo mirrors on the south.
Both spawns have four spread points and immediate cover so no one dies in the first two seconds.

The inner killhouse is the map's power position — a 30 m × 15 m building (sideways, long axis east-west) laid out as a maze
of 6 rooms in a 2×3 grid, with a big central tower (6 m × 6 m, +6 m) in the dead center. Every room has cover inside (barrels,
crates, low walls, pallets, dumpsters) and 2–3 doorways connecting it to adjacent rooms, so no single defender can lock it
down. The central tower has 4 entries (N, S, E, W) and gives 360° view from the top. Two catwalks run along the east and
west warehouse walls at +4 m, giving rotational verticality — they exist for flanking, not camping.

DESIGN PILLARS
    3-lane flow. Each lane has a distinct personality (CQB / long-range / vehicle). Players can commit to a lane or rotate via
    cross-passages at the north and south action zone.
    Symmetric balance. Alpha and Bravo spawns are mirror-imaged. No side gets a shorter rotation to the mid tower.
    Power position is contestable. The mid tower has four stair entries and is exposed from the catwalks. Holding it requires
    team support, not a single camper.
    Headglitch-friendly cover. Cars, low walls, and concrete barriers are placed at chest height so players can shoot over
    them while crouching — the core CoD cover loop.
    Fast movement routes. Catwalks and stacked containers provide mantle and climb paths. No dead-ends — every cover
    cluster has a flank exit.
    Light as info. Two skylight strips cast hard-edged pools on the action zone. Players crossing them are visible from the
    tower top and the catwalks.


   G A M E P L AY N O T E
   The skylights are open roof frames with safety grating, not glass. They cannot be shot through but they silhouette anyone crossing the
   light pool below. The central tower (inside the sideways killhouse) is positioned between the two skylight strips so a player holding the
   tower top is backlit from both spawns. The 30 m building blocks the direct spawn-to-spawn sightline through the mid lane — attackers
   must go around or through the killhouse maze, creating natural engagement zones on the north and south approaches.

SIX ZONES


Zone-by-Zone Breakdown

   1. Team Alpha Spawn — North                                                                                      10 M × 100 M

   Four Alpha spawn points spread across the 100 m width, each behind a car or low wall so players don't die in the first 2
   seconds. A dumpster anchors the center for mantle play. The spawn zone is open enough to push into any of the three lanes
   immediately.


   2. West Lane — Close-Quarters Aisles                                                                               25 M × 50 M

   Crate stacks and pallet rows create winding aisles for SMG and shotgun play. A forklift and dumpster provide mid-height cover,
   and a wood wall subdivides the lane. Barrel clusters near the south approach give shoot-through cover. Two cross-passages
   connect to the mid lane — one north, one south — so flankers can rotate without backtracking.

   3. Mid Lane — Long Sightline + Tower                                                                               50 M × 50 M

   The main engagement lane. A 50 m sightline runs from Alpha spawn to the tower's north face, and from the tower's south face

   tower's long walls. Tower holders can shoot both approaches but are exposed from four stair entries and two catwalk bridges.

I M P L E M E N TAT I O N G U I D E


Godot 4.6.3 Scene Notes
Build notes for Godot 4.6.3. The map is one main scene with nested child scenes per zone, so a designer can hide/show
zones without unloading the whole warehouse.

Scene Tree Structure

   Warehouse_Main (Node3D)
   ├─ Shell/                  Floor (100×70m concrete) + Walls_N/S/E/W (8m steel) + Roof_Trusses
   ├─ Skylights/              2× Area3D + OmniLight3D + VolumetricFog god-rays
   ├─ Lanes/
   │ ├─ West_Lane/            Cover_Group (pallets, crates, forklift, dumpster, barrels, walls)
   │ ├─ Mid_Lane/             Cover_Group (containers, cars, barriers, dumpsters, barrels) + Mid_Tower
   │ └─ East_Lane/            Cover_Group (trucks, barriers, container, dumpster, fence)
   ├─ Inner_Killhouse/        KillhouseBuilding.tscn (30×15m sideways, 6-room maze) + CentralTower.tscn (6×6m, +6m) +
   Room_Doorways ×10 + Room_Cover ×6 + Entries ×4
   ├─ Catwalks/               West_Catwalk + East_Catwalk (+4m) + Stairs ×4 (2N + 2S)
   ├─ Spawns/
   │ ├─ Alpha_Spawn/          SpawnPoint ×4 (team=ALPHA) + Cover_Group
   │ └─ Bravo_Spawn/          SpawnPoint ×4 (team=BRAVO) + Cover_Group
   ├─ Lighting/               WorldEnvironment + DirectionalLight3D (baked) + ReflectionProbe ×4
   ├─ Navigation/             NavigationRegion3D (baked navmesh, includes tower + catwalks)
   └─ TDM_Manager             score, spawn rotation, round timer



Geometry — CSG vs Imported Mesh

    Shell + lane dividers + tower structure: CSG boxes for greybox — fast iteration, collision auto-generates with
     use_collision = true . Promote to glTF meshes once layout is signed off.
    Cars, trucks, dumpsters, containers, crates, pallets, forklifts, barrels: Always imported glTF meshes. Reuse one
     Car.tscn for all 7 cars on the map, one Container.tscn for all 5 containers, etc.

    Inner killhouse: Two scenes — KillhouseBuilding.tscn (30×15 m sideways outer building with 6 rooms in 2×3
    grid, 10 internal doorways, 4 outer entries) and CentralTower.tscn (6×6 m tower in dead center, +6 m, 4 entries
    N/S/E/W, 4 top-floor windows, snap Marker3D on top). Each room gets its own cover instance (barrels, crate, low wall,
    pallet, dumpster).
    Catwalks: One Catwalk.tscn with the walkway mesh, railing mesh, and stair collider. Instance twice (E and W walls).
    Skylights: Open rectangles in the roof mesh + transparent emissive panel below for the light shaft. No glass — open
    frames with safety grating only.

Collision Layers

    8 layers: World, Cover, Player, Enemy, Projectiles, Triggers, Navigation, Skylight Shaft. Cover splits into Full
    (containers/tower/trucks — block all), Low (barriers/cars — headglitch, block body below 1.2m), Shoot-Through
    (barrels/fences/pallets — block movement only). Bullet raycast respects the split.

TDM Logic, Navigation & Performance

    TDM spawn manager picks spawn by team + min-distance-from-enemy. If all spawns within 20m of enemy, fall back to
    danger spawn with 2s invulnerability. Symmetric — Alpha and Bravo use the same logic.
    Anti-spawn-trap: 3 lanes fork immediately out of each spawn, so campers cannot lock a single exit. Spawn cover gives
    headglitch defense without trapping players.
    Mid tower contest: 4 stair entries + 8 window frames. Any player on top is exposed from ≥3 angles. Area3D triggers
    "Tower Held" callout when one team has 2+ players for 5s.
    Catwalk rotation: 1.5m wide, no cover, waist-high railings. One-way mantle at tower bridge.
    TDM score: +100/kill, +25 for tower hold (per 5s). First to 100 kills or 5-min timer wins.
    Navmesh + Lighting: NavigationRegion3D over 100×70m floor; separate regions on tower top + catwalks via Link3D.
    VolumetricFog 0.04–0.06; SDFGI low; skylights 3–5× ambient.

Free Texture Site Guide
All four sites below are free, allow commercial use, and provide PBR texture sets (albedo, normal, roughness, AO, height,
metallic). No per-asset crediting required, and most offer bulk downloads so you can pull an entire category at once.


   1. Poly Haven                                                                                               polyhaven.com/textures

   CC0 · 8K MAX · PBR FULL SET · FREE FOR COMMERCIAL USE

   The gold standard. Every texture is a complete PBR set with up to 8K resolution. Use their bulk download page to grab entire
   categories. For this MW2019 warehouse: concrete (floor + barriers), corrugated metal (walls + shipping containers), plywood (crates +
   pallets), and steel (catwalks + tower). They also have HDRIs for the skylight lighting setup.
